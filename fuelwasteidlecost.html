This dashboard mirrors the Battery Intelligence layout and adds fuel, idle, cost, and CO2 visibility with editable assumptions.

Calculation Logic

  • Total fuel used: summed from the Geotab FuelUsed entity across the last 6 months.
  • Idle fuel used: uses Geotab TotalIdlingFuelUsedL when present. If that value is missing, the dashboard estimates idle fuel using idle hours multiplied by the editable idle burn rate.
  • Idle cost: calculated from idle fuel used multiplied by the editable fuel price. Fuel price can be entered per gallon or per liter.
  • CO2 produced: calculated from total fuel volume using an emissions factor tied to fuel type. Gasoline uses 8.887 kg CO2 per gallon. Diesel uses 10.180 kg CO2 per gallon.
  • Worst offenders: ranked by idle cost across the last 6 months, then by idle hours when costs are tied.
  • Trend windows: the chart groups the last 6 months into monthly buckets so the movement is easy to compare at a glance.
