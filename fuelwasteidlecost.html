How the numbers are built

  • Total fuel used: adds up fuel use across the last 6 months.
  • Idle fuel used: uses recorded idle fuel when available. If it is missing, the dashboard estimates idle fuel from idle time and the burn rate you set.
  • Idle cost: multiplies idle fuel by the fuel price you choose.
  • CO2 produced: estimates emissions from the fuel used and the fuel type you select.
  • Vehicle ranking: puts the biggest idle-cost opportunities near the top of the list.
  • Trending: compares this month's current pace with last month so you can quickly see whether costs are heading up or down.
