Status Logic

  • Needs Attention: used for sustained recent weakness, not one-off dips. It typically requires a low recent average or multiple warning signals together, such as a high weak-read rate combined with worsening trend.
  • Watch List: used when early warning signs are present but the overall recent average is still fairly normal. This is often the right status for vehicles that show occasional weak starts or mild deterioration.
  • Healthy: shown when the recent average, weak-read rate, and recent trend do not suggest a sustained problem.
  • Weak read: any cranking-voltage reading at or below 10.2 V during the recent 45-day window.
  • Deep low events: repeated reads at or below 9.4 V add weight, but they no longer create a Needs Attention status by themselves. They must agree with the recent average, weak-read rate, or regular-use pattern.
  • Inactivity context: low-voltage events are compared with trip gaps. If most weak reads happen after 7 or more idle days and very few happen after normal use, the recommendation is softened and the detailed review explains why.
  • Vehicle review: the detailed view turns the data into a plain-English story, shows the recent voltage pattern, highlights usage activity, and gives you a printable export for customer discussions.
